The Engineer

I started Dean Engineering for a simple reason: I love houses — and the people who dream them up. Watching an idea become drawings, then a frame, then a home somebody lives their life in never gets old, and engineering the part that holds it all up is the best seat in the house.

That’s why this firm doesn’t feel like a call center. Every project gets a licensed engineer who reads the plans, runs the numbers, and picks up the phone when you have questions. Whether you’re a homeowner sketching an addition on a napkin or a builder who needs a beam sized by Friday — we’d love to be part of the dream you’re building.
